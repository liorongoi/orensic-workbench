## Clone detection and noise map (real photographs)

Generated by `python -m forensic.validation run --only phase4 --pristine <folder>` on six real photographs from the
scikit-image sample data (astronaut, coffee, chelsea, rocket, motorcycle_left, motorcycle_right), each manipulated at
seeded random places (60 cases per condition). Six photographs is a small corpus: repeat with the lab's own images.

The noise map is a visual aid first: natural noise varies with focus, texture and JPEG, so its outlines are
prompts to look, and it is not part of the Smart Report battery. Copy-move detection is.

Full results, conditions and known failure modes are in the detector cards (`backend/forensic/validation/cards/`), which the app shows next to each detector and prints in reports.

## Geometric checks and the generated-image detector

Generated by `python -m forensic.validation.geomgen`.

**Shadows, reflections, perspective** (seeded synthetic constraint sets, default tolerance 2° plus
2 px click error): no false conflicts on consistent sets with 1 to 2 px click error (reflections
3 % at 2 px). A constraint turned by 20° (an object under another light, or in another
perspective) is found in 84 % (shadows), 85 % (reflections) and 88 % (perspective) of sets, over
90 % with five or more constraints, about 50 % at 10° and under 15 % at 5°.

**Generated-image detector** (Community Forensics ViT-S/16-384), measured on a 4,769-image sample
of the Community Forensics evaluation set (20 generators held out from training; real images from
LAION, ImageNet, FFHQ and COCO), threshold 0.5:

|---|---|---|---|
| As given | 0.842 | 0.004 | 0.977 |
| JPEG 90 (both classes) | 0.716 | 0.004 | 0.963 |
| JPEG 75 (both classes) | 0.644 | 0.004 | 0.953 |

By generator: 94-100 % for open diffusion models (FLUX, Stable Cascade, Kandinsky, LCM, DeciDiffusion),
GANs (DFGAN, GALIP) and Midjourney 5.2; 76-89 % for DALL-E 2/3, Imagen 3 and Ideogram 1; 35-65 % for
Firefly Image 2/3, Midjourney 6.1, Ideogram 2 and Hourglass. Real images in the sample are mostly
